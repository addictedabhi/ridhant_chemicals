About Ridhant

Bridging global chemistry

Ridhant Speciality Chemicals is a rapidly emerging company that has made an impressive mark in the industry. We serve industries including polyolefins, plastics, construction, paints, hot melt adhesives, paper, ink and coatings, personal care and lubricants. We supply high-quality speciality chemicals and additives, together with the technical expertise that helps customers improve product performance, optimise processes and find cost-effective solutions.

With a strong commitment to quality, reliability and customer satisfaction, we bridge the gap between global chemical manufacturers and end users through customised products, application support and value-added technical services.
