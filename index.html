About Ridhant

Bridging global chemistry and Indian manufacturing

Ridhant Speciality Chemicals is a trading company specialising in solutions for the plastics, rubber, construction, paper, paints, road-marking paint, food and allied industries. We supply high-quality speciality chemicals and additives, together with the technical expertise that helps customers improve product performance, optimise processes and find cost-effective solutions.

With a strong commitment to quality, reliability and customer satisfaction, we bridge the gap between global chemical manufacturers and end users through customised products, application support and value-added technical services.
