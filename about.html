About us

Bridging global chemistry

Ridhant Speciality Chemicals is a rapidly emerging company based in Kota, Rajasthan, supplying speciality chemicals, additives and technical expertise across the globe.

Who we are

Ridhant Speciality Chemicals LLP is a rapidly emerging company that has made an impressive mark in the industry. We are dedicated to providing high-quality speciality chemicals and technical solutions to industries including polyolefins, plastics, construction, paints, hot melt adhesives, paper, ink and coatings, personal care and lubricants.

Our portfolio covers metallic stearates, 12-HSA salts, liquid dispersions, PE and PP waxes, antioxidants, fillers and minerals, food additives, stabilisers, antistatic additives and oxo-biodegradable additives, with other speciality additives sourced on request.

We supply products from trusted manufacturers, and we also offer the technical expertise that helps customers improve product performance, optimise processes and find cost-effective solutions. Our role is to bridge the gap between global chemical manufacturers and end users through customised products, application support and value-added technical services.

Our goal is to build long-term partnerships founded on consistent quality, technical expertise, competitive pricing and dependable customer service.

Our vision

To become a trusted partner for industries seeking advanced materials, sustainable solutions and continuous innovation.

Our mission

To supply consistent, specification-grade chemicals on time, and to stand behind every delivery with technical support.
