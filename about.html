About us

Connecting global chemistry with Indian manufacturing.

We're a speciality chemicals trading company based in Kota, Rajasthan, supplying additives and technical expertise to manufacturers across India.

Who we are

Ridhant Speciality Chemicals LLP serves the plastics, rubber, construction, paper, paints, road-marking paint, food and allied industries. We supply high-quality speciality chemicals and additives, and we also offer the technical expertise that helps customers improve product performance, optimise processes and keep costs under control.

We bridge the gap between global chemical manufacturers and end users with customised products, application support and value-added technical services, so the right molecule reaches the right process.

Our vision

To be the trusted partner for industries seeking advanced materials, sustainable solutions and continuous innovation.

Our mission

To supply consistent, specification-grade chemicals on time, and to stand behind every delivery with technical support.
